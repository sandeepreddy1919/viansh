About Viansh Art Studio & Workshops

📍 HYDERABAD

Viansh Events is a creative event company dedicated to bringing people together through engaging art workshops, craft workshops, DIY stalls for experiences, gifting solutions, and interactive event activities. We design meaningful experiences that inspire creativity, encourage learning, and provide hands-on opportunities for individuals, families, schools, colleges, corporate teams, and communities. From painting, resin art, pottery, and handmade crafts to DIY stalls, customized gifting, festive celebrations, and experiential events, Viansh Events creates memorable moments that blend creativity with practical learning. Meet the Founder

📍 HYDERABAD

Hi, I'm Anusha, the founder of "Viansh Events". Viansh Events was born from a simple thought—I wanted to attend events that offered more than just an activity. I was looking for experiences where I could learn, experiment, create, and walk away with something meaningful. When I realized there was a gap in such experiences, I decided to create them myself. Creativity has always been my passion, and my vision is to build a community where people can come together to explore new skills, express themselves, and connect through thoughtfully curated workshops and events. From art and craft workshops to DIY experiences and creative community events, every experience is designed to inspire learning, encourage creativity, and make a positive impact. \My goal with Viansh Events is simple—to create meaningful experiences that bring people together and add real value to the communities we build.
